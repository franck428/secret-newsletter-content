CONVERSATION HELP

Common subscriber questions

Why does it cost $4.99 per month?

The subscription saves readers research time by delivering a curated shortlist of 10 surprising and useful discoveries twice a week instead of an endless shopping feed.

Is the first month really free?

The offer includes a 30-day free trial. No Freelancer commission is earned during the trial. Afterward, the subscription continues at $4.99 per month unless cancelled.

Can subscribers cancel?

Yes. The subscription can be cancelled. Never suggest that a subscriber is locked in.

What will subscribers receive?

They receive curated editions featuring 10 surprising, useful and accessible technology discoveries, with links to complete product information.
